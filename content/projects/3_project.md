  Aiming at the demand for intelligent badminton training, this project has successfully developed an autonomous mobile badminton training robot. The system integrates a LiDAR and a depth camera, fuses autonomous navigation, visual perception and multi-source data fusion algorithms, and can realize core functions such as dynamic serving, automatic returning, quantitative training evaluation and virtual opponent interaction. It constructs a closed-loop intelligent training system, effectively breaking through the functional limitations of traditional training equipment, and achieving the goal of low-cost and efficient training. As a professional training equipment integrating intelligence and multi-functions, the robot adopts a dual friction wheel structure and a pan-tilt mechanism to achieve full-angle serving, and is equipped with an Internet of Things (IoT) architecture to support real-time data feedback, covering three core markets: professional training, education systems and public fitness.

  <strong style="font-size: 16px; display: block; margin: 16px 0 8px 0; color: #ebcb8b;">My Responsibilities:</strong>

  I was mainly responsible for the construction of the robot's perception system, the design of the automatic decision-making mechanism, and the implementation of the path search trajectory smoothing algorithm and real-time obstacle avoidance strategy. In the project, the Fast-LIO algorithm was adopted, combined with the mid360 radar to complete the SLAM mapping and positioning tasks; the ego-planner algorithm was deployed in the path search and trajectory smoothing link, and its dimension was optimized by simplifying 3D planning to 2D. Finally, the core functions of automatic ball searching and precise serving of the badminton robot were realized, which effectively guaranteed the stability and reliability of the system's motion control.
